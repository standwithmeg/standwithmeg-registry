# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in Stand With Meg, please report it privately. **Do not open a public GitHub issue.**

**Contact:** founder@standwithmeg.com

Please include:

- A description of the issue and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce
- Affected URLs, endpoints, or components
- Your name or handle for credit (optional)

We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days and provide a status update within 10 business days.

## Scope

In scope:

- my.standwithmeg.com and standwithmeg.com
- The Next.js application in this repository
- Authentication, authorization, and data-handling code paths
- Supabase integrations and row-level security policies

Out of scope:

- Denial-of-service testing
- Social engineering of Stand With Meg staff or volunteers
- Physical security
- Third-party services not operated by Stand With Meg

## Sensitive Data

Stand With Meg handles sensitive personal information submitted by families affected by the family court system. Reports involving potential exposure of:

- Survey respondent data
- Identifying details about minors
- Court case information

...should be treated as high priority and sent directly to founder@standwithmeg.com with `[URGENT]` in the subject line.

## Safe Harbor

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who:

- Report vulnerabilities in good faith
- Do not access, modify, or delete data beyond what is minimally necessary to demonstrate the issue
- Give us reasonable time to respond before public disclosure
- Do not exploit the issue for personal gain
