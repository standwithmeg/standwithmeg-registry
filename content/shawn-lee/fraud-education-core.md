# Shawn Lee Report — core teaching (Q&A context)

## Reframe
Some families may be victims of crime and going to the wrong place for relief — paying family lawyers for motions when the question may be a law-enforcement complaint.

## Wire fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1343) — elements in plain English
1. Scheme to defraud or obtain money/property by false pretenses
2. Intent to defraud
3. Wire in interstate commerce (email, Zoom, e-filing, billing, transfer)
4. Wire furthered the scheme

## Mail fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1341)
Bills in the mail for services not provided or recklessly fraudulent may be relevant depending on the facts.

## Honest services / scheme to defraud (18 U.S.C. § 1346)
Paying a professional to file false information can deprive someone of honest services — depending on the facts.

## Influencing an officer (18 U.S.C. § 1503)
Corruptly endeavoring to influence an officer in the discharge of duty — up to 10 years depending on prosecution.

## § 1001
Knowingly false statements to federal investigators are a separate federal crime. We teach accuracy, not ammunition.
